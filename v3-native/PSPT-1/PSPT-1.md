# PSPT-1

## Product
PSPT-1

## Description
The PSPT-1 is a handheld inline device used with the Range Finder Toroid while the pumps are running normally.

## Purpose
The PSPT-1 is used to adjust GAIN and ATTENUATION so that HIGH and LOW limits can be forced to trip during testing.

## Important distinction
The PSPT-1 is a test and adjustment device. It does not measure or calibrate the Range Finder Toroid.

## Related equipment
The PSPT-1 is used with the Range Finder Toroid.
